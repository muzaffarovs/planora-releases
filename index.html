Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Planora is new and its installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Each release lists the installer's SHA-256 on GitHub if you'd like to check the file.

Is it free?

Yes. The Windows app, the Android app and the Telegram bot are free.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the file on your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time: that's normal for apps from outside the Play Store. Then open Planora and continue with Telegram. It tells you when there's a new version.

How do updates work?

Planora checks for new versions by itself and downloads them in the background. When one is ready, Settings shows "Restart to update".

Do I need the bot to use the app?

No. You can use Planora on one computer without an account. Signing in with Telegram adds reminders on your phone and keeps several computers in sync.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Planora → Uninstall. Your account stays; delete it from the app's Account page or with /deleteme in the bot.
